Our story

Serious about sangas

Scott's is a little luncheonette on the Gold Coast Highway in Palm Beach. We make proper sandwiches: real ingredients, cooked on the flat-top, toasted and wrapped while they're hot.

What goes in

Meat, bread and veggies

Our meat

From local butchers

Marinated rib fillet, slow-cooked brisket and pulled pork, house-cured corned beef for the Reuben, and crispy fried chicken. Our meat comes from premium local butchers.

Brisket and pulled pork are slow-cooked in-house. Everything else is cooked to order.

Our bread

Toasted when you order

Every sanga is toasted fresh. Swap to Grande ciabatta or sourdough for $3, or gluten-free for $5.

Our veggies

Cut in-house every morning

Fresh onions, capsicum, tomatoes, rocket and lettuce, prepped every morning. Our pickles, slaws and sauces are made here too.
