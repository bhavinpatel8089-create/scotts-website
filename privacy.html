What we collect

When you create an account or order online we collect your first name, email, mobile number and your order history (items, totals, points). Card payments are handled by Square; we never see or store your card details.

Why we use it

  • To run your Scott's Rewards account: points, free sangas and the $3 Sanga of the Week.
  • To prepare your order and let you know when it's ready.
  • To stop misuse of offers (for example, one $3 sanga per mobile number each week).
  • To send you Scott's deals, only if you ticked the box. You can unsubscribe any time.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run our ordering and rewards: Square (payments and orders) and Supabase (account database). We don't sell your details.

Your choices

You can ask to see, fix or delete your details at any time. Contact us on 0473 460 362 or at the counter. If you have a privacy complaint you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
