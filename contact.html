How does order-ahead pickup work?

Order online, choose a pickup time and pay. Collect it from the counter under your name.

Do you have gluten-free bread?

Yes, any sanga can go on gluten-free bread for $5. Our kitchen handles gluten, so we can't guarantee there's no cross-contact.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. The 'No Chicken' Sanga uses a plant-based fillet and vegan mayo, and our cold-pressed juices are vegan.

What about allergies?

Tell us about any allergy when you order. Our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, egg, fish and peanuts.
